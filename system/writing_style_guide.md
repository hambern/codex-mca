# Writing Style Guide

## Principles
- **Simplicity**: Use short, declarative sentences.
- **Polysyndeton**: Use "and" to link clauses rather than commas, creating a rhythm of continuity.
- **Concrete Imagery**: Focus on the physical sensation. How the line cuts the hand. How the sun burns the neck.

## Dialogue
- Dialogue should be sparse but meaningful.
- Often repetitive, mimicking the rhythm of speech.
- No adverbs in dialogue tags. Just "he said" or "she said".

## Formatting
- Standard English quoting.
- Paragraphs focus on action sequences.
