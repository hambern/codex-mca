# Negative Constraints

## Forbidden
- **Sentimentality**: Do not tell the reader how to feel. Show the action that causes the feeling.
- **Flowery Prose**: No purple prose. No excessive metaphors.
- **Abstract Nouns**: Avoid words like "honor", "glory", "sacrifice" unless used ironically or in dialogue.
- **Internal Monologue**: Keep internal thought grounded in the immediate task or memory. No abstract philosophizing.
