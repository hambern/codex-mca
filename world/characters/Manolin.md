# Character Biography: Manolin

### Background
A young boy who fished with Santiago until his parents forced him to work on a "luckier" boat. He still visits Santiago daily to help him carry his gear and bring him food.

### Voice
Respectful, caring, eager to learn.
*Example:* "The hell with luck. I'll bring the luck with me."
