# Character Biography: Santiago

### Background
An old Cuban fisherman who has gone eighty-four days now without taking a fish. He was once a champion arm wrestler ("El Campeón") in his youth. He lives alone in a shack.

### Voice
Simple, respectful, often talks to himself or his hands.
*Example:* "Fish, I love you and respect you very much. But I will kill you dead before this day ends."
