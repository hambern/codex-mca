# Lore: The Sea

### La Mar vs El Mar
Santiago always thought of the sea as **La Mar** which is what people call her in Spanish when they love her. Sometimes those who love her say bad things of her but they are always said as though she were a woman.

Some of the younger fishermen, those who used buoys as floats for their lines and had motorboats, spoke of her as **El Mar** which is masculine. They spoke of her as a contestant or a place or even an enemy.

### The Gulf Stream
The "great well" where the shrimp and bait fish congregate, leading the big fish there.
