# Story Outline

## Act 1: The Salao
- Santiago has gone 84 days without a fish.
- Manolin is forced to leave him but still cares for him.
- Santiago resolves to go out "far out" into the Gulf Stream.

## Act 2: The Struggle
- Santiago hooks the great Marlin.
- The three-day battle of endurance.
- Santiago kills the Marlin and lashes it to the skiff.
- The shark attacks begin. Santiago fights them off until he has nothing left.

## Act 3: The Return
- Santiago returns to the harbor with only the skeleton.
- He sleeps, exhausted.
- The other fishermen marvel at the skeleton.
- Manolin promises to fish with him again.
- Santiago dreams of lions on the beach.
